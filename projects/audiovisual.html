Composing Audiovisual Space

Affiliation Manhattan School of Music - Ades Performance Space - Piano+ Performance Group
Type Creative Coding, Sound Synthesis & Lighting Design
Role Creative Technologist / TouchDesigner Artist
Collaborators Jixue Yang (pianist) · Ya-Lan Chen (composer)
Year 2025
Tools Live-action shooting, TouchDesigner · Adobe Premiere Pro

How can the relationship between sound, moving image, and live performance express the tension between meditative stillness and inner struggle?

In collaboration with pianist Jixue Yang and composer Ya-Lan Chen, I developed a live audiovisual performance presented at Ades Performance Space in New York, Trinity Episcopal Church in Houston, and Indiana State University in Terre Haute.

The work approaches meditation as a shifting negotiation between tranquility and unrest. A fish drifting through dark, swirling water serves as a metaphor for the individual psyche, while water vibrations echo Buddhist humming to establish an underlying pulse. Moments of synchronization and divergence between sound and image explore how alignment and disruption shape the experience of stillness.

The installation combines semi-transparent gauze with layered projections to place the pianist between the fish and its shadow. This spatial arrangement brings the performer into the projected environment, blurring the boundaries between body, image, and reflection. Together, these elements invite the audience to experience meditation as an unstable passage between calm, suspension, and entrapment.
